# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our team and
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community — and to build a product (Kusanya) that treats its users,
Kenyan SME exporters and their buyers, with the same respect.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning
  from the experience
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the team and the users
  we serve

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their
  explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Hackathon-specific commitments

Under deadline pressure we additionally commit to:

1. **Respect the tie-break:** when debates stall, the demo script (`docs/solution.md` §15)
   and the deadline decide; whoever "loses" the call supports the team fully.
2. **Credit work honestly:** in submissions, decks, and repos — including AI-assisted work
   and third-party rails (Payaza, TypeSafe, shadcn/ui). We do not present mocked flows as
   live money movement.
3. **Protect user data in demos:** fixtures and seeds use synthetic personas; no real
   merchant, buyer, or phone data appears in slides, recordings, or logs.
4. **Keep it safe:** no commit contains secrets, and no demo moves real funds without
   explicit, informed consent (see `SECURITY.md`).

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Team leads are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable
behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior
that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all team spaces (repository, chats, calls, in-person at
Hackhouse Nairobi) and also applies when an individual is officially representing the team
in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the
team leads at the contact email in `README.md` (or privately to any lead). All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. All team leads are obligated to
respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
